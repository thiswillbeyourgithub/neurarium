## Added

- Search now finds drug-metabolising enzymes: typing CYP2D6, or UGT, or an isoform's full name opens its panel, with the drugs on both sides of it. There is a matching Enzymes filter chip. Until now the only way to an isoform was to scroll its section.

Une pastille de filtre Enzymes l'accompagne. Jusqu'ici, le seul chemin vers une isoforme était de faire défiler sa section.
