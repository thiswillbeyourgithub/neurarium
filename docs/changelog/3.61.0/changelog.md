# 3.61.0 (2026-09-07)

## Added

- Four medicines against nausea, vertigo and motion sickness join the roster: metoclopramide, granisetron, meclizine and dimenhydrinate. They act on receptors the brain map already carried, so each arrives with its measured affinities, its half-life and its sourced mechanism. Metoclopramide is worth a look: the dopamine blockade that settles nausea is the same one that can, over months, cause a movement disorder. (6f638d8)

Ils agissent sur des récepteurs que la carte du cerveau contenait déjà, et arrivent donc chacun avec ses affinités mesurées, sa demi-vie et son mécanisme sourcé. Le métoclopramide mérite le détour : le blocage de la dopamine qui calme les nausées est celui-là même qui peut, au fil des mois, provoquer un trouble du mouvement.
- A medicine that is a chemical pairing of two others can now say so under its own name. Dimenhydrinate, the motion-sickness pill, is diphenhydramine bonded to a mild stimulant, so its panel points at both rather than claiming measurements that belong to another molecule. (d09dfa5)
